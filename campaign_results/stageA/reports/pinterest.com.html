8. Limitations

  • Automation fingerprint: sites may behave differently under Playwright-driven browsers.
  • Single vantage point (one host/region) — regional comparisons require tagged repeat runs.
  • HttpOnly cookies are invisible to in-page capture; cookie figures are lower bounds.
  • Foxhound does not taint IndexedDB reads; IDB→network provenance comes from the in-page hook layer.
